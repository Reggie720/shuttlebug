# Thunderbolt (1947): content review — HOLD

Reviewed November 26, 2026. Not added to the public catalog or Roku package.

## Identity and sources

- AFI film record: https://catalog.afi.com/Film/25017-THUNDERBOLT
- Official National Archives presentation: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U-5T8E-LaI8
- National Archives record: https://catalog.archives.gov/id/63897
- FedFlix transfer metadata: https://archive.org/metadata/gov.archives.arc.63897
- Transfer master: https://archive.org/download/gov.archives.arc.63897/gov.archives.arc.63897.mpeg

The film has an introduction by James Stewart. The theatrical release is 1947; production/archive references also use 1945. It is a World War II documentary, not a fictional James Stewart feature.

## Checks performed

- Downloaded and decoded the full official-transfer 320x240 MP4.
- Downloaded and decoded a separate 656x480 MP4, duration 2508.96 seconds.
- Compared full-film audio envelopes: correlation approximately 0.99685, aligned within 50 milliseconds. This supports identity, not clearance.
- Visually inspected twelve frames of the sharper MP4: an added watermark appears across the program. That version was rejected.
- Sampled the MPEG-2 master: 720x480 video and DVD PCM audio. Inspected opening credits, including the original introduction credit for James Stewart.
- The original credit frame visibly states: "COPYRIGHT 1947 BY MONOGRAM PICTURES, INC. AND CARL KRUEGER PRODUCTIONS."

## Release decision

HOLD. The official archive description says access and use restrictions are unrestricted, but the on-screen third-party copyright notice needs title-specific resolution. Do not treat custody, an uploader's CC0 label, or audio identity as a sufficient distribution grant.

Before enabling: resolve copyright status for this exact edition and intended territories, including renewal and incorporated material; retain evidence; obtain a clean browser/Roku-compatible master with original audio and credits; review the complete master and playback; add an accurate combat/death advisory. Do not label archival SD as HD.

No distributor agreement, fee, purchase, or new streaming permission was accepted. No new movie or episode was published during this review.
