# Shuttlebug original house media

Original text/shape graphics generated for A Hog Entertainment. No third-party movies, stock footage, music or synthetic voices used.

- Shuttlebug_Promo_20s_1080p.mp4: completed 20-second silent promotional spot, 1920x1080, H.264 video / silent AAC track, 30 fps. Four five-second title cards and opening/closing fades. Promotes the actual website, not an unapproved Roku launch.
- Shuttlebug_Ident_6s_1080p.mp4: completed six-second silent brand ident in the same format.
- promo-poster.jpg: frame from the original promo.
- Second_Shift_Pilot_Script.md: original fictional one-room live-action shooting script. Not a finished movie or series episode.
- Rebuild promos with `python3 tools/make-house-promos.py` (FFmpeg + DejaVu fonts).

House promotion is unpaid. Files are deliberately not activated as pre-rolls, listed as movie episodes or presented as paid sponsor advertisements. Review the clips on /studio.html. Actors, locations and any added music/footage need appropriate permissions before filming or releasing the pilot.

## Deep music bumpers

Owner supplied Deep.mp3, confirmed ownership, requested promotional cutouts and approved the quiet ten-second version. Only the approved short excerpt is published; original full track remains private.

- Deep_Bumper_10s_Quiet.mp4: 1280x720 web copy of the approved waveform bumper; music reduced by approximately 12 dB from initial promo, gentle fades.
- Deep_Dance_10s.mp4: original procedurally modeled/animated 3D character, 1280x720 composite at 24 fps, with the same quiet ten-second music excerpt. Character rendered at 512px, interpolated and enlarged into the composite. No existing character or celebrity likeness used.
- tools/make-dance-ident.py: Blender procedural character and animation source. Set DANCE_FRAME_DIR to choose frame output. Default renders 48 frames at 12 fps for a four-second loop. Final clip loops/interpolates the animation and uses the approved soundtrack.

Clips are available for review/download on /studio.html. They do not automatically insert breaks into normal film playback, activate paid advertising or claim sponsor revenue.
