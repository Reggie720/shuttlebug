# Shuttlebug content requirements

The owner requires natural-sounding audio. Do not publish robotic-sounding voices, synthetic narration, text-to-speech scratch voices, or artificial dubs. Use original recorded performances and interviews, or films without spoken dialogue. Preserve the film's original soundtrack. Document the source of the recording or the publisher's no-dialogue statement before adding a new title.

The robotic “The Move” preview is canceled. Do not recreate it, resume its production, or restore its listing or media without a new explicit request. Archived notes are not an active production order.

Only publish complete titles with a documented distribution permission, an accessible and tested source, correct creator attribution, a license link, accurate runtime, and honest quality/language information. Do not turn an Archive search result or an uploader's label into automatic permission. Confirm rights with the creator's own release, official film credits or an authorized distributor.

No subscriptions, purchases, ad-network enrollments, new hosting resources or changes to paid plans without the owner's approval. Preserve the red-and-black design.
