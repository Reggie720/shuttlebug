Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains the current information practices of Shuttlebug, operated by A Hog Entertainment, on our website and Roku application. Shuttlebug offers free streaming. No Shuttlebug account, subscription or payment details are required.

Information stored on your device

The website uses browser local storage for your watchlist, viewing progress, TV layout preference and optional advertising choice. Its service worker caches application pages and interface assets for loading and limited offline navigation; streaming still needs an internet connection. An exported watchlist is downloaded to your device.

The Roku app stores favorites, resume positions and a recently watched list in that device’s Shuttlebug registry. The recently watched list is limited to 50 distinct titles. Our app does not upload these local records to a Shuttlebug account or analytics database. Devices and browsers have separate records; there is no account-based synchronization.

Connection information and external content

When you load pages, artwork, captions or videos, the hosting and content-delivery providers receive network requests. These can include your IP address, requested resource, request time, browser or device headers and referrer information where sent. The website is hosted through Render. Media and artwork may load from providers such as Blender, SideWays, Wikimedia Commons and Internet Archive. Their handling of requests is governed by their own policies.

The Roku app also requests Shuttlebug’s content-rights register over HTTPS to verify permissions before allowing playback. This creates a hosting request, rather than uploading your local favorites or history. The current app code does not request your precise location, contacts, microphone, camera, Roku account email. Roku separately operates its platform and may process information under its own privacy policy.

Advertising and measurement

Shuttlebug is intended to be advertising-supported. As of the effective date, paid video advertising, Google display ad loading and direct sponsorships are disabled in the deployed configuration. The Roku 0.3.2 update enables Roku Advertising Framework audience-measurement configuration and supplies the current title’s ID, genre and duration. Ad targeting is restricted through the framework’s limit-ad-tracking setting. Roku and its measurement services may process device or advertising identifiers under their own privacy controls and policies; Shuttlebug does not store those identifiers in its local history or a Shuttlebug analytics database. No paid ad demand is configured.

Optional developer test commercials are labeled “TEST ADVERTISEMENT — NO PAID REVENUE.” They use Shuttlebug-hosted test resources on the web and a packaged clip on Roku. They do not request paid demand or send ad impression/tracking beacons. Ordinary website requests for test resources still reach the hosting provider.

The website includes an optional advertising preference. Choosing Allow currently does not activate ads while publisher configuration remains disabled. Declining does not block viewing. Before third-party advertising is activated, this policy and the applicable advertising/consent controls must describe the actual partners and data uses. Google’s policies are available at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Messages you send us

If you email support, a title request, a privacy request or a copyright concern, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Title requests open your own email application; typing a request alone does not submit it to a Shuttlebug form server. We use correspondence to respond, handle the request and administer the service. Business email is handled through Gmail.

Storage, sharing and your choices

Local records remain until cleared or removed by you, your browser or device; storage availability is not guaranteed. You can remove web records through your browser’s site-data controls. Website Settings also offers viewing-progress clearing and watchlist export. Roku data is separate and is managed through that device’s app storage. We cannot remotely read or erase records held only on your device.

Support correspondence remains in our business email unless deleted. Hosting, media and email providers control their own logs and retention settings; we do not promise a fixed deletion period for their records. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us, or to ask about privacy rights that apply to you. Please provide only what is needed to identify the request, never a password or payment information. Applicable legal obligations can affect how a request is handled.

The current application does not transmit your local watchlist or viewing history to advertisers or sell those records. Network requests necessarily disclose connection information to the providers delivering the service. Clicking an external link takes you to another provider’s service.

Children and household viewing

The catalog may include animation and other material viewed by families; this does not mean every title is suitable for every age. The current app has no child accounts, age-verification flow or parental-consent collection system. Please do not send us personal information about children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has supplied personal information can contact us at the address below so we can review the concern. This policy does not claim that a child-directed advertising service has been approved.

Changes and contact

We will post changes here with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact A Hog Entertainment at ahogentertainment@gmail.com.
